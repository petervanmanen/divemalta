## Description (max 4000)

Dive Malta is a map of the dive sites of Malta, Gozo and Comino that works completely offline. Plan your dives at home, check a site on the boat or at the shore, and look up the depth anywhere — without mobile data.

92 DIVE SITES
From the Blue Hole and the Inland Sea at Dwejra to the Um El Faroud, the Ċirkewwa arches, Santa Marija Caves and the wrecks at Xatt l-Aħmar. Each site shows what you need to choose a dive:
• Maximum and typical depth
• Level: Beginner, Advanced, Experienced or Technical
• Type: wreck, cave, reef, statue
• Shore or boat entry, and how easy the shore entry is
• Conditions: best wind direction, currents, things to watch out for
• A short description and the sources it is based on
• Directions to the site in Apple Maps

DEPTH CONTOURS
Depth lines at 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 80, 100, 150 and 200 m show the shape of the seabed around all three islands: where the drop-offs are, how far out the 30 m line lies, where the reefs and plateaus are.

DEPTH ANYWHERE
Touch and hold the sea to see how deep it is at that spot.

WORKS OFFLINE
The map, the dive sites and the depth data are all inside the app. No account, no downloads, no mobile data needed — ideal on the coast or out at sea.

SEARCH AND LOCATION
Search all dive sites by name, and see where you are on the map when you're in Malta.

PRIVATE
No account, no tracking, no ads. Your location is only used on your phone to show where you are.

ABOUT THE DATA
The map is based on OpenStreetMap. The depths come from the EMODnet Bathymetry model of the European seas, combined with the OpenStreetMap coastline. Dive site information was researched from dive guides and local dive clubs, and is shown with its sources.

Depths, levels and conditions are a guide only. The depth model shows the shape of the seabed, not every rock, and can differ from the real depth close to cliffs. Always check with a local dive centre and dive within your training and limits.

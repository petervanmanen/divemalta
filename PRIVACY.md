# Dive Malta — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Dive Malta does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no ads.** The app has no sign-in and contains no
  tracking or analytics code.
- **Location.** If you tap the location button, the app asks for permission to use your location
  while it is open. Your location is only used on your device to show where you are on the map. It
  is never sent anywhere or stored.
- **Works offline.** The map, the dive sites and the depth data are included in the app. The app
  does not contact any server to show them. Links you tap yourself (a dive site's website, its
  sources, OpenStreetMap, or directions in Apple Maps) open in Safari or Maps, which have their own
  privacy policies.
- **Settings.** The app only remembers on your device whether the depth contours are switched on.
